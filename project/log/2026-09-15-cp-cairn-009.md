# CP-CAIRN-009 — the kit and the documentation plane

Opened 2026-09-15 on the owner's go-ahead in the chat, registered directly
on the trunk as `3c42237`, governed by the 1.1 page, path 5's journal
entry and fourteen records at their blob ids — all sixteen byte-identical
at the candidate. Closed the same day at candidate `0e7c0b1`, the fourth.
Eight units, each pushed and green. The opening acceptance was never
amended: the scope digest at the candidate equals the one accepted at
registration.

## What it did

The kit installs what 1.1 decided. `init` declares
`transport.registration: manual-git` from one constant, `--transport`
answering the integration transport alone, and the generated binding
prints both from the configuration's own declaration (ADR-024). The
bootloader names six skills and carries two absolute rules beside the five
of 1.0 — an abstraction explained persists as a concept note, an
explanation is written for the reader who is learning it (ADR-011 d3,
ADR-021 d1, ADR-022 d2).

The documentation plane it writes is the one 1.1 decided: `docs/inputs/`,
a concept root of three folders — `cairn`, `product`, `learning` — each
with its index and none at the root, an architecture index, a
documentation index that maps the plane and states where a surface page
goes, and a module note template that describes only now (ADR-010 d1,
ADR-011, ADR-012, ADR-019 d2, ADR-023). `cairn/README.md` is generated at
`init` and every `update` and written by no hand (ADR-013). `update`
rewrites every pristine file whoever owns it, prints what a release
changed in an edited one from Git's own diff, and hands over one named
file on `--take` (ADR-015). The manifest carries the post-mortem tool and
names Ponytail at `v4.9.0` without copying it (ADR-014 d1, ADR-016 d1);
the generated workflow runs once per commit that can land and the
generated request template opens with the three plain lines (ADR-005,
ADR-018 d2, ADR-021 d2). `concept-orphan` and `concept-growth` read the
whole root through its folders. Chapters 3 and 6, the concepts and the
layout say the same in the specification's voice.

## What the closing found, and what that says

The candidate was rebuilt three times, and only the first was a mistake.

**The first was the writer's.** A brainstorm note was pushed to the trunk
while the path was `ready`, which moved the trunk under a green
candidate and fired `rebase`. The drift was benign — the delta touched
neither `writes:` nor `governs:` — but a candidate is an identity, so it
was rebuilt. The lesson is not about the note: it is that a note parks at
a unit boundary, not during closure.

**The second was the owner's try.** `cairn init` into an empty folder,
read by the owner before the merge as ADR-001 d3 requires, showed the
pointer page listing six chapters over one URL. `outwardLinks` had passed
it every time, because it proves a link resolves and six identical links
all resolve; the defect lived in a dimension nothing compared, the targets
against each other. S08 anchored each chapter at its own heading and the
test now checks every anchor against the headings `spec/index.md`
carries.

**The third was the review on the request**, on a candidate already
green. Three defects, one destructive: `update --take package.json`
overwrote a real product manifest with the kit's template, because the
guard read the fresh plan instead of the lock and `init` drops
`package.json` when the adopter already has one. It printed *nothing else
was touched*. Beside it, the to-reconcile list evaporated on a second
update against the pointer page's own promise, and the scripts notice
named three scripts while the generated manifest carried four. S09 fixed
all three, each reproduced before and after.

Two of those three are the protocol's own steps working on a candidate the
writer and the gates had already passed. That is the argument for keeping
both.

## What it did not do

Four of the roadmap register's five milestone rows read `running` for
paths that are done. The status is hand-kept there while each path's
record already carries `status:`, and nothing reconciles the two; row 6
does not own the register either. Named in S06 and raised, not patched:
outside this path's acceptance.

The review movement could not be performed as written for five of the
eight units. `cairn-unit` movement 4 offers three readers and assumes one
works; sixteen of twenty fresh contexts hung, each emitting one line or
none. S03 to S06, S08 and S09 were read by the writing context and every
one says so in its step rather than implying a verdict nobody gave. The
observations are parked in
[the brainstorm note of 2026-09-15](../brainstorm/2026-09-15-the-review-movement.md),
and the owner has ruled the missing fallback gets a record in its own
path.

One advisory stood at the candidate and was accepted with its records
named: `decision-drift`, the architecture folder changing without a
record, for ADR-019 d2 and ADR-023 d2 and d3 — accepted a week before this
path opened.
