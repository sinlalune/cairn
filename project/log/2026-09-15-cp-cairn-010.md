## What the closing found, and what that says

The path's own definition of done contradicted itself, and the
contradiction was read at the candidate rather than fixed. Item 5 asked
that the two superseded decisions be marked in their records' status
lines; item 7 asked that the same two records be byte-identical to their
pinned blobs. The marks were made, item 7's clause was stated as
knowingly not met in the request's description, and the owner merged on
that reading. It is observation 3 of the first feedback file — a
definition of done contradicting a record it cites — met on the path
that wrote the file, against its own item. The change that file asks
for, the open skill reading each item against the record it names before
the go-ahead, would have caught it at registration.

One other reading was made alone and recorded. The definition of done
named `githubSlug` as gone from the checker; the post-mortem imports it.
ADR-029 moved the two functions rather than break the tool, S03 widened
`writes:` by the post-mortem, its test and the workflow test, and the
register's row followed in the same unit.

## What it leaves

GitHub's all-zeros sentinel is meant; the catalogue's prose for the rule
says GitHub. ADR-029 decides the reading, not that rule's wording, and it
was left as it was, named in S03. The four changes the first feedback
file asks for — a placeholder in a record's surface name put to the owner
at registration, a measured figure written once and pointed at, each item
of a definition of done read against its record before the go-ahead, a
word *pending* in a record's implementation table — are asked, not made,
and no row owns them yet.
