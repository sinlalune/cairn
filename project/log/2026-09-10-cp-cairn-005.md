## Protocol violated during closing

The administrative commit of ADR-008 decision 3 was not made. `ready` and
`subject_commit` should have been declared on the branch after acceptance
and before the merge; instead the writer told the owner the commit would
follow the merge, and the owner merged a path that stood at `running`.
Nothing was lost: the candidate is exact, its check was read green on the
commit that landed, and the merge commit `9457cf6` records who merged and
when. The trunk therefore takes this path from `running` to `done` in one
commit, on the edge ADR-001 decision 7 removes and the checker still
allows because path 2 has not landed. Once that edge goes, this shape is
refused and the remedy is the one ADR-001 names: declare `ready` on the
branch first. No history is tidied to hide it.
