# CP-CAIRN-013 — forward plan

Read when planning, never before every step. Each item is one work unit.
A unit that changes a skill changes its reference and the chapter or
concept that gives the same order in the same commit, so no two pages of
the specification disagree between units.

1. **S01 — the open skill and the configuration reference.** The two
   reads before the go-ahead in step 2 (ADR-030); the precondition
   sentence before step 3 and the `pull-request` registration sequence in
   step 3 and the reference, each under its declaration (ADR-032 decisions
   1 and 2); step 1 reading `project/backlog/` (ADR-041) and naming the
   definition of done a plain list (ADR-042); the configuration
   reference's `init` paragraph and `transport` row.
2. **S02 — the close skill's order and its sentences.** Steps 3 and 4
   swapped, with the execution protocol's closure list, chapter 5 and the
   administrative-closure concept following (ADR-040); the measured-figure
   sentence (ADR-031 decision 1); the `tools/soundness.md` sentence gone
   (ADR-034 decision 8); the `deferred` disposition naming a backlog file
   (ADR-041); the checkbox clause gone (ADR-042); the two release-path
   sentences (ADR-038 decision 3, ADR-039).
3. **S03 — the coherence questions as a fresh-context read.** The close
   skill's steps 2 and 3 and the reference; the human-records reference's
   *Coherence* section; chapter 5's `full` route sentence; the
   coherence-audit concept naming the reader; this repository's request
   template's *Coherence* list with its first line (ADR-043).
4. **S04 — the unit skill and the seventh skill.** Section 7's route for a
   note about Cairn (ADR-038 decision 2) and movement 4's `deferred`
   disposition (ADR-041); `skills/cairn-update/SKILL.md` in ADR-035's
   order, and the bootloader's skills line.
5. **S05 — the template, the chapters and the layout.** The path
   template's plain list (ADR-042); chapters 1 and 4 naming
   `project/backlog/` (ADR-041); the layout reference's rows for the
   backlog, for `feedbacks/` with its release subfolders and its type, and
   for `CHANGELOG.md` (ADR-038 decisions 3 and 5, ADR-039, ADR-041).
6. **S06 — the candidate.** Trunk merged in, gates bare, the register's
   row 1 of 1.2 naming this path; then the close skill as this path leaves
   it: the administrative commit, the fresh-context coherence read in the
   request's description, the owner's try, the merge.

## What the units must not do

Name a record in a skill's sentence — the skills say what to do; the
records say why, and stay where they are. Change a rule, a tool or a kit
file — a sentence that needs one names the surface the register's later
row will make true.
