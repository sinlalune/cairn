# CP-CAIRN-016 S07

### S07 — a stale register cell is reported, never refused — **COMPLETE**

#### Plan

The administrative commit on `47d3292` was refused before it was made:
setting `status: ready` and running `cairn-active` rewrote row 4's cell
to *ready*, and the register may not change after the candidate
(`acceptance`); leaving the register out made `cairn-active --check`
exit 1 on the stale cell, which the checker's `derived-view` reads as a
stale view. S01 had built, through that exit code, the checker rule on a
state cell that ADR-031 lists among its rejected alternatives — and no
closing commit, here or at an adopter, could pass. This unit makes
`--check` say a stale cell and keep its exit code for the view alone,
and places the rest — the closing commits carrying the cells — on the
backlog. `47d3292` is void; this unit's commit is the new candidate.

Definition-of-done item advanced: the first, corrected — *reports a
cell that disagrees with the records as it reports a stale view* is kept
as a report, without the refusal ADR-031 rejected; and the eighth, whose
closing was blocked.

Type `implementation`: the wiring test changed first — a stale cell
under a current view exits 0 and prints its line — and failed before the
change.

This is not put to the owner as a decision: ADR-031 already made it, in
its rejected alternatives.
