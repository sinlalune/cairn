# CP-CAIRN-001 S01

### S01 — the specification on the six stages, cut before added — **COMPLETE**

#### Plan

Move `docs/cairn/specification/` to `spec/` at the repository root, the place
the convergence record's section 4 gives it. Write `spec/index.md` on the six
stages of the chronology, with the 0.2 normative text folded into chapter five
and cut to what the manifesto keeps. Merge the concept pages section 4 lists.
Delete before adding at every step. Measure the words at the end.

Deliberately not in this unit: the rule cut (S02), the path-record shape and
the routes in the checker (S03), the pull-request transport in the
configuration (S04). The specification is written to the 1.0 shape first; the
tools follow in their own units, and the conformance page says so.

- **Moved** the specification tree with `git mv` to `spec/`. Deleted its
  `log.md`: the owner retired folder logs in the specification.
- **Wrote `spec/index.md`** new, on the chronology: chapters 1 to 4 (idea,
  research, vision, roadmap) are light shapes with almost no rules; chapter 5
  is the coding cycle, the 0.2 text cut from 17,455 words to its rules; chapter
  6 is the learning loop with the three-scope wiki, learning notes and the
  writing rule. It states the 1.0 shape the owner ruled: opening acceptance
  inline, a resume section instead of a brief, pull-request closure with
  `manual-git` as fallback, `depends_on:` and the unblocked view, two routes,
  no rewriting by default, the unit as plan → change → self-review → verify.
- **Moved the conformance matrix, the generated linkage and the rule
  catalogue** out of the index to `spec/reference/conformance.md`, verbatim,
  under a preface that says it is the 0.2 matrix describing the 0.2 tools this
  repository still runs, and that every row naming a shape the specification no
  longer has is a pending cut. The old conformance checklist on that page was
  deleted: it restated the matrix. The page also carries the weight-budget
  table, to be filled at release (S08). `cairn-rules` now writes there.
- **Merged concepts** as section 4 lists: six state pages into
  `lifecycle.md`; six role pages into `roles.md`; three finding pages into
  `finding.md`; the five soundness pages into `tools/soundness.md`, out of the
  wiki, with the index's "prove that the gate can fail" section folded in.
  Deleted `foundation-path.md` and `emergency-path.md`: the specification no
  longer names those routes (`foundation` folds into `full`, `emergency` is
  dropped), and a concept nothing links is an orphan. `route.md`,
  `coding-path.md`, `lightweight-path.md`, `work-unit.md` and `scope-digest.md`
  were edited to match. `checkpoint-retention.md` stays until S02 retires its
  rule: the reference pages still link it.
- **Rewrote `spec/concepts/index.md`**: 21 borrowed terms, 31 own concepts.
- **Repointed every link** to the moved tree and the merged pages across
  `spec/`, `docs/`, `project/`, `AGENTS.md`, `cairn.config.json`
  (`roots.concepts` is `spec/concepts`).
- **Tools, minimal**: `cairn-rules` writes into the conformance page;
  `cairn-check`'s Markdown corpus adds the parent of the concept root, so a
  specification outside `docs/` is link-checked and counts for
  `concept-orphan`; `cairn-init`'s portable-docs constant and generated texts
  name `spec/`; the workflow runs the gate on pushes to `path/**` as well as
  the trunk, so CI can be read after every unit.

  the configuration named and the seeded kit never wrote; `same-work-unit`
  requires it when `tools/` changes.

  preface that dates it.

- Debt noted for S06: `cairn.lock.json` still lists the 86 files at their 0.2
